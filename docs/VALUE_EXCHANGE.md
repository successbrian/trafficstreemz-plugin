# The Value Exchange

This document exists so there's never a question about what this free
plugin costs and what it gives.

## What you get (free)

- A working AI prospecting system for three income engines
  (Zilara, GotBackup, My Lead Gen Secret)
- Guided setup: enrollments, Gmail, funnels, training
- Brian's personal help getting it configured

## What Brian gets

1. **Your contact details** (name, email, WhatsApp) — captured at
   install, with your explicit on-screen consent, so he can help you
   set the system up. Not sold, not shared, not spammed.

2. **Affiliate positioning** —
   - TrafficStreemz: he's pre-filled as your enroller, but you can
     change it. Your business, your choice.
   - Systeme.io: if you're new, you join under him. That's the
     price of the free funnel templates.

## Why this is fair

A free AI system you can't set up is worthless. Brian's help *is*
the product as much as the code. Your contact info is what lets him
deliver it. The affiliate links are how a free plugin sustains
itself — the same way free WordPress plugins have a pro upsell,
except here the "upsell" is just joining the businesses through him.

No dark patterns. No silent data collection. Everything above is
stated in the installer before you type a single character.
